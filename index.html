About Me

I am an aspiring HR specialist with a background in Psychology. I am especially interested in how people think, how they make decisions, and how this shows up in communication, motivation, and teamwork. I focus on building strong fundamentals in sourcing, candidate screening, and candidate experience. My goal is to grow step by step and collect real practical experience, not only theory from courses.

Why I Want to Work in HR

I like the idea that HR is not only about processes, but also about people's real stories. For me, HR is where psychology, communication, and business meet. I enjoy listening to people, understanding what they are looking for, and trying to see how they could fit into a team or company.

I want to grow in HR because I see it as a place where I can combine empathy with structure: helping candidates feel respected during the process, while still thinking logically about requirements, skills, and expectations of the company.

My HR Learning Logbook

A simple log of what I'm currently practicing and thinking about in HR. I update it as I learn, reflect, and complete new small projects and tasks.

Week 1 · Sourcing Basics
Understanding how to read job descriptions
This week I focused on really understanding job descriptions instead of just scanning them. I tried to separate must-have skills from optional ones, and to see what kind of person the company is actually looking for beyond formal requirements.
Week 2 · Candidate Experience
Rewriting cold rejection messages
I took several typical "cold" rejection templates and rewrote them in a way that feels more human. I wanted to keep them short but still respectful, clear, and kind. It helped me think more from the candidate's perspective.
Week 3 · Communication Practice
Short, clear HR messages
I started practicing how to write short HR-style messages: invitations to screening, follow-ups, and simple check-ins. The goal was to keep them professional, yet friendly and easy to understand.
